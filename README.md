## Delivery 1: Executive Understanding Report

Addressed to the Chief Executive Officer and the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), dated 20 August 2026. The report establishes which frameworks, regulations, and ethical principles govern GlobalTech's client engagements across government, banking and fintech, healthcare, and industry sectors.

### Frameworks and Standards Reviewed

| Framework / Standard | Issuing Body | Jurisdiction |
|----------------------|--------------|--------------|
| NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 | NIST, U.S. Department of Commerce | United States / international |
| Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 | Federal Republic of Nigeria / NDPC | Nigeria |
| ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (ISMS) | ISO / IEC | International |
| PCI DSS v4.0.1 | PCI Security Standards Council | International |
| HIPAA Security Rule | U.S. HHS, Office for Civil Rights | United States |
| UK GDPR | UK Parliament / ICO | United Kingdom |
| Cybercrimes (Prohibition, Prevention, etc.) Act 2015, as amended 2024 | Federal Republic of Nigeria | Nigeria |
| CIS Critical Security Controls v8.1 | Center for Internet Security | Global |
| COBIT 2019 | ISACA | Global |
| CBN Risk-Based Cybersecurity Framework for DMBs and PSBs | Central Bank of Nigeria | Nigeria (banking and payments) |

Each entry documents the client types governed, the core requirement, why it applies to GlobalTech's engagements, and its source.

### Ethics and Professional Conduct

Maps the ISC2 Code of Ethics canons to GlobalTech's internal policy:

- Protect society, the common good, public trust, and infrastructure
- Act honorably, honestly, justly, responsibly, and legally
- Provide diligent and competent service
- Advance and protect the profession
- Supporting principles on public trust and ethical accountability

### Compliance Note and Oath

Sets out how the intern will apply ISO/IEC 27001, PCI DSS, and the NDPA in daily work, and closes with a signed oath of compliance covering confidentiality, authorized access, and responsible conduct.

## Delivery 4: Judgement Reflection

A reflective response to a scenario in which a senior analyst downplays frameworks and policies in favor of technical skill. It argues that professional standards and technical proficiency cannot be separated, drawing on the CIS Controls v8.1 and Canon I of the ISC2 Code of Ethics.

## Topics Covered

- Security frameworks: NIST CSF 2.0, ISO/IEC 27001, CIS Controls v8.1, COBIT 2019
- Data protection law: NDPA 2023, UK GDPR
- Sector regulation: HIPAA, PCI DSS, CBN cybersecurity framework
- Professional ethics: ISC2 Code of Ethics
- Compliance, client confidentiality, and responsible conduct
